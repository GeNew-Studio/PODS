# OPTN database — dropdown lists (heart and lung)

**Source:** [OPTN Database](https://www.hrsa.gov/optn/data-calculators/about-optn-data/optn-database)  
**Page last reviewed:** December 2025

This file records the categories named on that page. Each heading is a dropdown. The items under it are the entries the page names. The page does not publish coded option values, so this file does not add any.

Donor and recipient forms in the app keep their own fields. This list is the OPTN category map for heart and lung.

## Scope

- Current organs: heart and lung, kept separate.
- Heart-lung combination appears on the page as an organ type. It is out of scope for this project.
- Later organs, names only: kidney, liver, pancreas, intestine.

The page says much of the data are collected on electronic forms. Other data come from the Patient Waiting List, Feedback Records, and Match Run programs.

## Forms named on the page

### Pre-transplant

- Transplant Candidate Registration Form
- Deceased Donor Registration
- Living Donor Registration Form
- Donor Histocompatibility Form
- Transplant Recipient Histocompatibility Form
- Potential Recipient Form
- Feedback Record (online file created after the Match Run)

Living Donor Feedback is the service the transplant program uses at the time of a living-donor transplant. UNet then generates the data collection forms.

### Post-transplant

- Transplant Recipient Registration Form (organ-specific; generated after the transplant and after feedback)
- Transplant Recipient Follow-Up Form

### Waiting list — who may enter data

- UNOS Organ Center staff
- Registered UNOS transplant professional members

### Waiting list — non-renal candidate items

The page groups these under non-renal candidates. Heart is in that group. Status codes are stated for heart and for liver. Lung is not given a separate item list; it is covered here only as a non-renal candidate.

- Patient status code, reflecting degree of medical urgency (stated for heart and liver)
- ABO blood group
- Patient age
- Gender
- Number of previous transplants
- Acceptable donor characteristics

Adding a patient generates a Transplant Candidate Registration Form. Completing that form adds further clinical data about the potential recipient. The page does not list those further fields.

Renal waiting-list items (name, gender, race, age, ABO blood group, HLA) belong with kidney and are not expanded here.

### Match run — inputs to the rank order

A match run compares one deceased donor organ with candidates on the waiting list and returns a rank-ordered list of potential recipients.

- Organ allocation policies
- Transplant center acceptance criteria
- Local variances (OPTN-approved variations of OPTN allocation policies)

### Donors — registration type

### Donors — living donor items

- Donor OPO
- Donor demographics

### Histocompatibility — form

- Donor Histocompatibility Form
- Transplant Recipient Histocompatibility Form

### Histocompatibility — donor items

- OPO information
- Donor HLA typing
- Relationship of donor to recipient (living donor)
- Degree of haplotype match (living donor)

### Histocompatibility — recipient items

- Transplant center information
- Recipient HLA typing
- Recipient PRA level
- Crossmatch data
- Non-local donor HLA retyping

### Potential transplant recipients

After a deceased donor organ is allocated, the donor OPO starts a Potential Recipient Form.

- Partial rank-ordered list of potential recipients, taken from the Match Run printout
- Refusal code, entered for each person ranked above the actual recipient, for the reason the organ was not accepted

The page does not list the refusal-code values.

### Feedback record — who enters what

The Feedback Record is the first confirmation that a transplant was performed.

- OPO or its representative: the donor, and the organs recovered for transplantation
- Recipient transplant center or its representative: the actual organ recipient, for each organ

After those entries, UNOS generates the next forms.

- OPOs receive the donor forms
- Transplant centers receive the recipient forms
